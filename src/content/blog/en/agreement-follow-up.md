Signing closes one stage, but it does not always finish the work. Many agreements include terms, deliveries, payments, renewal notices or other obligations that someone must follow.

## Keep the agreement with its context

Alongside the final file, record the parties, the responsible team, the related case file and its status. This makes an agreement easier to find without relying on a personal folder or the name used to save a PDF.

An [agreement repository](/en/features/agreement-management/) can link a contract to its document and the signing envelope that produced it. Use consistent tags and fields so search remains useful as the portfolio grows.

## Turn relevant clauses into tasks

Important dates and commitments are more useful when someone owns them. For each obligation, define what needs to happen, who is responsible and when it is due. If completion needs support, keep the evidence with the obligation record.

There is no need to turn every sentence in a contract into a task. Prioritize commitments that require an action, decision or future review.

## Set notices before a due date

A renewal date can go unnoticed if it only appears inside a PDF. Record how far in advance the team should be notified, whether the agreement renews automatically and who must decide whether it continues.

Advance notice gives people time to review terms, budgets and needs before a deadline. If the agreement should not continue, its owner can start the required actions in time.

## Keep amendments connected

When the parties agree to a change, link the amendment to the original contract. That makes it easier to review what changed, when it became effective and which document supports the new term without losing the earlier history.

## Review the portfolio

A portfolio view helps find agreements nearing expiration, overdue obligations and documents still waiting for an action. An agreement timeline adds context when someone needs to reconstruct a decision or check an event.

Signing and follow-up are parts of the same lifecycle. Keeping context, assigning owners and planning around dates turns a signed file into an agreement the team can manage.
